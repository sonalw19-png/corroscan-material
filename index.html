About the Project

Corrosion, AI-based detection, and the technology behind CorroScan.

What Is Corrosion?

Corrosion is the gradual degradation of metals through chemical or electrochemical reactions with their environment, most commonly seen as rust on iron and steel. It costs the global economy over USD 2.5 trillion annually (about 3.4% of global GDP) and poses safety risks in infrastructure, aerospace, marine and industrial sectors.

Types of Corrosion

  • 🌊 Uniform – attacks the whole exposed surface evenly.
  • 🕳️ Pitting – small, deep pits that can cause hidden structural failure.
  • ⚡ Galvanic – dissimilar metals in electrical contact in a corrosive environment.
  • 🔩 Crevice – confined gaps and joints with differing oxygen concentration.

Why AI-Based Detection?

  • ✅ Consistent assessment, instant severity measurement
  • ✅ Scales to thousands of images; supports drone/camera footage
  • ✅ Automated PDF reports for records
